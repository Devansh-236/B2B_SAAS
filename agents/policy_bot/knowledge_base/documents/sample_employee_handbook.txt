1. COMPANY OVERVIEW

TechCorp Solutions is a leading technology company committed to innovation and excellence. We value our employees as our greatest asset and are dedicated to creating an inclusive, supportive work environment.

2. EMPLOYMENT POLICIES

2.1 Equal Opportunity Employment
TechCorp Solutions is an equal opportunity employer. We do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, gender, age, national origin, disability, or any other protected characteristic.

2.2 At-Will Employment
Employment with TechCorp Solutions is at-will, meaning either the employee or the company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause or notice.

3. WORK ARRANGEMENTS

3.1 Remote Work Policy
TechCorp Solutions supports flexible work arrangements to promote work-life balance and productivity.

Remote Work Eligibility:
- Employees must have been with the company for at least 6 months
- Performance must be satisfactory or above
- Role must be suitable for remote work
- Manager approval is required

Remote Work Guidelines:
- Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week
- Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM EST
- Employees must be available for meetings and collaboration during business hours
- Home office must meet security and productivity standards
- Regular check-ins with manager are required

3.2 Working Hours
Standard working hours are 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday. Flexible start times are available with manager approval, allowing employees to start between 8:00 AM - 10:00 AM.

3.3 Overtime
Non-exempt employees are eligible for overtime pay at 1.5 times their regular rate for hours worked over 40 in a workweek. Overtime must be pre-approved by a manager.

4. BENEFITS AND COMPENSATION

4.1 Salary and Payroll
Employees are paid bi-weekly on Fridays. Direct deposit is required for all employees.

4.2 Health Insurance
TechCorp Solutions provides comprehensive health insurance coverage for employees and their families. The company covers 80% of the premium cost, with employees responsible for the remaining 20%.

4.3 Retirement Benefits
401(k) Plan: Company matches employee contributions up to 6% of salary. Vesting occurs over 4 years.

4.4 Professional Development
Annual professional development budget of $2,000 per employee for courses, conferences, and certifications.

5. LEAVE POLICIES

5.1 Vacation Time
- 0-2 years: 15 days per year
- 3-5 years: 20 days per year
- 6+ years: 25 days per year
- Vacation requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance
- Maximum of 5 consecutive vacation days without manager approval

5.2 Sick Leave
- 10 sick days per year
- Sick leave accrues monthly
- Doctor's note required for absences of 3+ consecutive days
- Unused sick leave does not carry over to the next year

5.3 Personal Leave
- 3 personal days per year for personal matters
- Personal days must be requested at least 48 hours in advance
- Personal days do not carry over to the next year

5.4 Parental Leave
- Maternity Leave: 12 weeks paid leave
- Paternity Leave: 4 weeks paid leave
- Adoption Leave: 6 weeks paid leave
- Additional unpaid leave may be available under FMLA

6. CODE OF CONDUCT

6.1 Professional Behavior
All employees are expected to maintain the highest standards of professional conduct. This includes:
- Treating colleagues with respect and dignity
- Maintaining confidentiality of company information
- Avoiding conflicts of interest
- Reporting unethical behavior

6.2 Harassment and Discrimination
TechCorp Solutions has a zero-tolerance policy for harassment and discrimination. All employees have the right to work in an environment free from harassment, discrimination, and retaliation.

Reporting Process:
- Report incidents to your manager, HR, or through the anonymous hotline
- All reports will be investigated promptly and confidentially
- Retaliation against reporters is strictly prohibited

6.3 Dress Code
Business casual attire is required in the office. Remote workers should dress appropriately for video calls. Casual Fridays are permitted with manager approval.

7. HEALTH AND SAFETY

7.1 Workplace Safety
All employees are responsible for maintaining a safe work environment. Report safety hazards immediately to your manager or HR.

7.2 Emergency Procedures
Emergency evacuation procedures are posted throughout the office. All employees must participate in quarterly fire drills.

7.3 COVID-19 Protocols
- Stay home if experiencing symptoms
- Follow CDC guidelines for isolation and quarantine
- Maintain social distancing when possible
- Use provided hand sanitizer and cleaning supplies

8. TECHNOLOGY AND SECURITY

8.1 Computer and Internet Use
Company computers and internet access are provided for business purposes. Limited personal use is permitted during breaks and lunch.

8.2 Password Security
- Passwords must be at least 12 characters long
- Include uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters
- Change passwords every 90 days
- Do not share passwords with anyone

8.3 Data Protection
- All company data must be stored on approved systems
- Personal devices are not permitted to access company systems
- Report any security incidents immediately to IT

9. PERFORMANCE AND DEVELOPMENT

9.1 Performance Reviews
Annual performance reviews are conducted in December. Goals are set at the beginning of each year and reviewed quarterly.

9.2 Career Development
TechCorp Solutions encourages career growth and development. Employees are encouraged to:
- Set career goals with their manager
- Take advantage of training opportunities
- Seek mentorship and coaching
- Apply for internal promotions

10. TERMINATION AND RESIGNATION

10.1 Resignation
Employees wishing to resign should provide at least 2 weeks written notice. Exit interviews are conducted with all departing employees.

10.2 Termination
Termination may occur for various reasons including performance issues, policy violations, or business needs. The company will provide appropriate notice and severance as required by law.

10.3 Return of Company Property
All company property, including computers, phones, access cards, and confidential information, must be returned upon termination.
