REMOTE WORK POLICY
TechCorp Solutions
Effective Date: January 1, 2024

1. POLICY OVERVIEW

TechCorp Solutions recognizes that remote work can enhance employee productivity, work-life balance, and job satisfaction. This policy establishes guidelines for remote work arrangements.

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

2.1 Employee Requirements
- Must be employed with TechCorp Solutions for a minimum of 6 months
- Must have a satisfactory or above performance rating
- Must have reliable internet connection and suitable home office setup
- Must be able to maintain regular communication with team and manager

2.2 Role Requirements
- Position must be suitable for remote work
- Job responsibilities must not require physical presence in office
- Must be able to access necessary systems and tools remotely

3. REMOTE WORK ARRANGEMENTS

3.1 Hybrid Work Model
- Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week
- Must be present in office for team meetings and collaboration days
- Core collaboration hours: 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM EST

3.2 Full Remote Work
- Available for select positions with manager approval
- Requires quarterly in-person meetings
- Must maintain regular video check-ins with manager

4. HOME OFFICE REQUIREMENTS

4.1 Workspace Standards
- Dedicated, quiet workspace free from distractions
- Reliable high-speed internet connection (minimum 25 Mbps)
- Proper lighting and ergonomic setup
- Secure storage for confidential documents

4.2 Technology Requirements
- Company-provided laptop and equipment
- VPN access for secure system connections
- Video conferencing capabilities
- Backup internet connection recommended

5. COMMUNICATION EXPECTATIONS

5.1 Availability
- Must be available during core business hours (9:00 AM - 5:00 PM EST)
- Respond to emails and messages within 2 hours during business hours
- Participate in all scheduled meetings and calls

5.2 Reporting
- Daily check-ins with manager via video call
- Weekly progress reports
- Monthly one-on-one meetings

6. PERFORMANCE AND PRODUCTIVITY

6.1 Performance Standards
- Same performance expectations as office-based employees
- Measurable goals and deliverables
- Regular performance reviews

6.2 Productivity Monitoring
- Time tracking for billable hours
- Project milestone tracking
- Regular feedback sessions

7. SECURITY AND CONFIDENTIALITY

7.1 Data Security
- Use company VPN for all work activities
- Secure storage of confidential documents
- No personal devices for work purposes
- Regular security updates and training

7.2 Confidentiality
- Maintain same confidentiality standards as office work
- Secure disposal of sensitive documents
- No unauthorized sharing of company information

8. EQUIPMENT AND EXPENSES

8.1 Company Equipment
- Laptop, monitor, keyboard, mouse provided by company
- Software licenses and subscriptions
- VPN and security tools

8.2 Home Office Expenses
- Internet service (up to $50/month reimbursement)
- Home office furniture (one-time $500 allowance)
- Office supplies (monthly $25 allowance)

9. TERMINATION OF REMOTE WORK

9.1 Company Right to Terminate
- Performance issues
- Security violations
- Business needs requiring office presence
- Violation of remote work policy

9.2 Employee Right to Return
- May request return to office at any time
- 30-day notice required for arrangement changes
- Manager approval required

10. POLICY UPDATES

This policy may be updated at any time. Employees will be notified of changes with 30 days notice.
